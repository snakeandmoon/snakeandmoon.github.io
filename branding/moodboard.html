A think tank exploring the post-Western digital economy.

Research. Foresight. Systems Architecture.

Global finance is fragmenting. BRICS Pay, the New Development Bank, and alternative payment rails are reshaping capital flows. Post-quantum encryption is racing against quantum decryption. Large computation models are hitting energy walls. And the next billion users are coming online through infrastructure that barely exists.

Snake and Moon maps these collisions. We produce the research that governments, funds, and multilateral institutions need to navigate the post-Western order.

Research Domains

1. GLOBAL FINANCE & PAYMENT SYSTEMS

The dollar-dominated financial order is fracturing. BRICS Pay, digital currencies, bilateral swap lines, and development banks are creating parallel infrastructure.

We analyze:

  • BRICS Pay architecture and adoption pathways.
  • The New Development Bank's role in African infrastructure finance.
  • Payment system fragmentation and regulatory arbitrage.
  • Sovereign digital currencies and their geopolitical implications.
  • The informal economy as a parallel financial system.

An Example Header

2. POST-QUANTUM ENCRYPTION

Quantum decryption will render current encryption obsolete. The transition to post-quantum cryptography is one of the most critical infrastructure challenges of the next decade—and emerging markets are the least prepared.

We study:

  • Post-quantum cryptographic standards and implementation timelines.
  • Energy-efficient encryption for low-resource environments.
  • National and multilateral readiness assessments.
  • Transition roadmaps for financial and government systems.
